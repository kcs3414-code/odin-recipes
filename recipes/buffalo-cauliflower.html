Buffalo Cauliflower

Description

This is a spicy, crispy baked cauliflower appetizer inspired by Buffalo wings. Bite-sized cauliflower is coated in a seasoned batter, baked until browned, then covered in a buttery hot sauce and baked again. It's great served with ranch or blue cheese for dipping.

Ingredients

  • olive oil cooking spray
  • 1 cup water
  • 3/4 cup gluten-free baking flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, or to taste
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 2 heads cauliflower, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup hot pepper sauce
  • 1 teaspoon honey

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees F (230 degrees C). Lightly grease a baking sheet with cooking spray.
  2. Whisk together water, flour, garlic powder, salt, and pepper in a large bowl until batter is smooth and somewhat runny.
  3. Add cauliflower pieces to batter and mix until evenly coated; spread onto the prepared baking sheet.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until lightly browned, 20 to 25 minutes. Set aside.
  5. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Remove from heat; stir in hot pepper sauce and honey until smooth.
  6. Brush hot sauce mixture over each cauliflower piece, repeating until all is used.
  7. Return to the oven and bake until cauliflower is browned, about 10 minutes. Let cool for 10 to 15 minutes before serving.
